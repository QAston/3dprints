Sailing clam cleat for rope by 22steve22 on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2152483

Summary:
I designed this working clam cleat for ropes of 4-6 mm. The overall size is 48x15mm and it can be fixed using two M4 countersunk screws the centre to centre distance of holes is 36mm. It is designed to be fixed on a plane or circular surface.For non sailors it works as a rope stopper.
