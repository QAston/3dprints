Hard Drive Cage | 1 - 6 Bays by Moonyx on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7146574

Summary:
 Info A compact hard drive cage for 3.5" drives that will fit most cases. Can be mounted to 120mm fan slots. Different models from 1 to 6 drive bays so you can choose which one best fits your needs or upgrade plans. Recommended to install it with rubber washers to reduce the noise from the drives. Printing Recommended to print using ABS or another material that can handle some heat.Print the caddy flat with support for the rails, handle and back edge.Cage can be printed without support with the back face on the print bed.
